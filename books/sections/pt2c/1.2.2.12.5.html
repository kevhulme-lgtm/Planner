1.2.2.12.5 EXPLAIN THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE FORMULAS FOR THE VARIANCE OF THE POPULATION AND A SAMPLE.

  1. A Sample Standard Deviation is calculated from only some of the data in a larger data set.
  2. Since the Sample Standard Deviation depends upon the sample, it has greater variability. So, the standard deviation of the sample is greater than that of the larger data set.
  3. If the data is a sample from a larger data set, we divide by one fewer than the number of data points in the sample, n − 1.
