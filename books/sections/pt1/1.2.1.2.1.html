Probability
1.2.1.2.1 DEFINE THE PROBABILITY OF A PARTICULAR OUTCOME.

  1. Probability is a measure of how likely something is to happen.
  2. The likelihood or probability of a given outcome is represented on a scale from the probability that the event cannot happen (zero probability) to when it is certain to (a probability of one).
