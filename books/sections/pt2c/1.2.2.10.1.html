1.2.2.10.1 EXPLAIN THE USE OF INTEGRATION FOR FINDING THE AREA UNDER A GRAPH.

  1. Integration can be used to find areas, volumes, central points and data for many other useful applications.

Example: A vehicle accelerates from rest to a velocity of 15 m/s in 6 seconds, maintains a constant velocity for 50 seconds and decelerates to rest in 9 seconds.
