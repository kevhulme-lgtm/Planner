1.2.2.11.3 CONSTRUCT AND INTERPRET STATISTICAL DIAGRAMS INCLUDING TABLES, CHARTS, GRAPHS AND PICTOGRAMS.

  1. All of the diagrams, graphs and charts can be constructed quickly and easily using programmes such as Excel, by entering the data into a table and selecting the appropriate type of diagram from the menu. However, it is important to understand the mathematics behind these programmes.
