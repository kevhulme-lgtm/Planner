Unit conversions
1.2.2.7.2 EXPLAIN THE CONVERSION BETWEEN MILLIMETRES, CENTIMETRES AND METRES FOR LINEAR, SQUARE AND CUBIC UNITS.

  1. When calculating areas and volumes, it may be necessary to convert between multiples of units.
  2. Care is needed, because the conversions into square and cubic units are not as simple as linear measure. It is easier to visualise the units if they are displayed on a cube:
