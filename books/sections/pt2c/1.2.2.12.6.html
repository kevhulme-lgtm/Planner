Sample standard deviation (summary)

Summary of the steps from Calculating the Mean through to finding the standard deviation.

Step 1: Calculate the mean of the data.

Step 2: Subtract the mean from each data point. These differences are called deviations.

Step 3: Square each deviation to make it positive.

Step 4: Add the squared deviations together.

Step 5: Divide the sum by one less than the number of data in the sample. The result is called the variance.

Step 6: Take the square root of the variance to get the standard deviation.
