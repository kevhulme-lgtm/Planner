The graph shows that the company would have to produce 47 items before it would be in profit.

This can be checked by formulating simultaneous equations from the text above:

  1. y = 1.85 x + 436
  2. y = 11.3 x + 0

As both of the expressions are equal to y, then they must be equal to each other. In this form the expressions can be equated by substituting one of them for y, as follows:

1.85 x + 436 = 11.3 x + 0

436 – 0 = 11.3 x – 1.85 x

436 = 9.45x
