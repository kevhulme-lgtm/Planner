The angle found by the calculator is 0° + 45°, so the next angle must be 180° – 45° = 135°. However, as the wave repeats again, the angles will be duplicated over the subsequent 360°. These remaining angles can be found by adding 360° to the first 2 solutions: 45° + 360° = 405° and 135° + 360° = 495°.

The values for these angles can be confirmed easily by using the sin function on the calculator: Sin 45°, Sin 135°, Sin 405° and Sin 495°
