Pythagoras’ Theorem

  1. “In a right-angled triangle, the square of the length of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the lengths of the other two sides”.
  2. This means that the area of the square drawn on side c in the diagram, is equal to the sum of the areas of the squares drawn on sides a and b.
