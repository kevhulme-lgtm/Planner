THE LAWS OF LOGARITHMS

  1. The laws of logarithms are similar to the laws of indices, and they enable logarithmic and indicial equations to be manipulated and solved. These laws are as follows.

The first two laws of logarithms only apply to logarithms with the same base:

1. Additions become Products

m n

The sum of logarithms with the same base can be combined to become a product

logbm + logbn = logb

2. Subtractions become Quotients

logbm – logbn = logb

The difference of two logarithms with the same base can be combined to become a quotient

3. Powers become Coefficients

Powers can be moved to the front to become multipliers

logbmn = n logb m

4. Inverse Variables become Negative Coefficients

An inverse variable in the function can be represented by a negative index
