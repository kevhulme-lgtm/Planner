  1. If a letter or symbol is squared or cubed, x2 or x3 then compared to x they are not like terms.

  1. It is not possible to reduce the expression 3a2 + 2a3 because the powers are different.

LOWEST COMMON MULTIPLE (LCM)

  1. The Lowest common multiple (LCM) is used extensively in all fields of business, engineering, management etc., to identify pinch-points in manpower or resources.

Example 1

A Land Rover in the Falkland Islands needs servicing every 4 months, a Lorry needs servicing every 6 months, and a Coach needs servicing every 9 months. At what point do they all require servicing at the same time?

The LCM is the smallest number into which each of the given numbers will divide exactly).

4 = 22 6 = 2 × 3 9 = 32

The LCM is the highest power of each of the variables:

22 × 32 = 4 × 9 = 36

So, the pinch-point when all of the vehicles will need servicing at the same time is at the 36-month point.

Example 2

Find the LCM for the following terms:

8a, 24a3, a3, 12a2

The LCM for the above terms is 24a3

This is because 24 is the lowest number into which all of the numbers will divide, and a3 is the lowest term of ‘a’ into which all of the ‘a’ terms will divide.

Example 3

Find the LCM for the following terms:

16abc, 4bc3, 8ac2, 32a2 b

The LCM for the above terms is 32a2 bc3

This is because 32 is the lowest number into which all of the numbers will divide, and a2 bc3 is the lowest of the terms into which all of the a, b and c terms will divide.

In the above examples, it is apparent that all of the numbers are factors of the highest number in the set of terms. If the LCM is not as easily identifiable, a factor tree can be used.

1.2.2.4.1 PLOT GRAPHS OF FUNCTIONS FROM EQUATIONS AND DATA.

  1. Diagrams are used in everyday life to represent data so that people can understand large quantities of information. The most common form of diagram is the graph. The basic components of graphs are as follows:

SCALE

  1. A scale is the relationship between the units used by a variable and its representation on the graph. The scale on a graph is specific to each situation and it is chosen to illustrate the data clearly. It refers to a set of numbers that indicate certain intervals on a graph used for measurement.

AXES

  1. This is plural for axis. The two perpendicular lines (vertical & horizontal) are each an axis. The horizontal line is always taken to be the ‘x’ axis (Independent variable) and the vertical line is the ‘y’ axis (Dependent variable)

INDEPENDENT VARIABLE

  1. This variable is often denoted by ‘x’ whose variation does not depend on that of another. Time or energy are often selected as an independent variable because other variables can often depend upon the time taken or the energy used. For example: the temperature of melting ice is dependent upon time elapsed, and the heat energy absorbed.

DEPENDENT VARIABLE

  1. This is a variable often denoted by ‘y’ whose value depends on that of another. For example, frictional force depends upon the load in many simple machines, so load would be plotted on the x axis as the independent variable and frictional force would be plotted on the y axis as the dependent variable.
