SIMPLIFYING ALGEBRAIC EXPRESSIONS AND EQUATIONS

  1. There are three laws which help us to simplify algebraic equations. These are:

It does not matter which way we add terms, or multiply terms:

3 + 2 is the same as 2 + 3

4 × 5 is the same as 5 × 4

  1. Commutative Law

a + b = b + a

a × b = b × a

This law does not apply to subtraction and division a – b ≠ b – a.

a ÷ b ≠ b ÷ a.

4 – 2 is not the same as 2 – 4

and

6 ÷ 3 is not the same as 3 ÷ 6

  1. Distributive Law

(a + b) c = ac + b c

This just means distributing (multiplying) the c into each of the terms in the bracket

c. Associative Law

(ab)c = a (b c)

(2 × 3) × 4 = 24

and

2 × (3 × 4) = 24

(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)

(3 + 5) + 4 = 12

and

3 + (5 + 4) = 12
