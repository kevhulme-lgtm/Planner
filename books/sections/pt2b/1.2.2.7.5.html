1.2.2.7.5 DESCRIBE HOW THE AREA OF A 2-DIMENSIONAL SHAPE BECOMES THE CROSS-SECTIONAL AREA OF A 3-DIMENSIONAL PRISM.

  1. the area of a face of any regular solid shape can be multiplied by the length of the shape to give the volume. This is true for simple, conjoined and complex shapes.

The volume of any regular prism is found using Cross-Sectional Area × length.
